1. Which Korean BBQ Meats Are Naturally Gluten-Free vs. Wheat-Marinated?

Korean BBQ is one of the easiest group dinners to make 100% Gluten-Free—if you know the golden rule between Unmarinated Cuts (생구이) and Soy-Marinated Cuts (양념구이):

  • ✅ 100% Naturally Gluten-Free Cuts: Chadolbagi (thinly sliced brisket), Samgyeopsal (unmarinated pork belly), Moksal (pork collar), Hangjeongsal (pork jowl), and Saeng-Galbi (unmarinated prime short rib). Ask for a clean grill grate and dip exclusively in Gireumjang (toasted sesame oil + sea salt + black pepper)!
  • ❌ High-Gluten Danger Cuts (at Restaurants): Beef Bulgogi, Yangnyeom Galbi (marinated short ribs), and Dwaeji Bulgogi (spicy pork) are marinated in brewed Korean soy sauce (wheat) and standard gochujang (wheat + barley malt).

3. Authentic 10-Minute Gluten-Free Bulgogi & LA Galbi Marinade Recipe

Hosting Korean BBQ at home lets you control every ingredient with zero cross-contamination on the grill grate. Blend these ingredients for 2 lbs of thinly sliced ribeye (Bulgogi) or flanken short ribs (LA Galbi):

  • 1/2 cup Certified Gluten-Free Tamari or Sempio GF Soy Sauce
  • 1/2 Asian Pear (or Bosc pear), peeled and grated (natural protease enzymes tenderize beef in 30 minutes!)
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar or coconut sugar + 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 6 cloves minced garlic + 1 tsp freshly grated ginger + chopped scallions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Korean BBQ safe for Celiac disease?

At a Korean BBQ restaurant, unmarinated meats (brisket, pork belly, unmarinated steak) dipped in sesame oil and salt on a fresh grill grate are naturally gluten-free. However, hosting Korean BBQ at home with Gluten-Free Tamari and O'Food GF Ssamjang is the safest way to avoid grill-grate cross-contact.

Does Ssamjang have peanuts or gluten?

Standard green-tub Korean Ssamjang contains wheat flour and barley malt, and some restaurant house-blends mix in peanut butter or crushed walnuts for richness. Always buy Chung Jung One O'Food Gluten-Free Ssamjang or mix GF Doenjang + GF Gochujang + sesame oil at home.

What is the sesame-free alternative to sesame oil for K-BBQ?

If you have a sesame allergy, use roasted Korean Perilla Seed Oil (Deulgireum / 들기름), which belongs to the mint family (Lamiaceae), or garlic-infused avocado oil with sea salt.
