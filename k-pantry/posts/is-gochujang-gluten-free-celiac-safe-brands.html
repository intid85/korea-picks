1. Is Gochujang Gluten-Free? The Hidden Wheat & Barley Malt Trap

Short Answer: No, standard Korean gochujang is NOT gluten-free—unless the tub is explicitly labeled Certified Gluten-Free. While centuries-old royal Korean gochujang was fermented strictly with glutinous rice powder (chapssal), fermented soybean powder (meju), red pepper powder (gochugaru), and salt, almost 85% of mass-market red tubs sold in US supermarkets use two hidden gluten ingredients to speed up fermentation and lower production costs:

  • Wheat Flour & Wheat Starch (소맥분 / 밀가루): Used as the primary carbohydrate base in standard CJ Haechandle, Sempio Classic, and Assi gochujang tubs.
  • Barley Malt Powder / Malt Syrup (엿기름 / 맥아엑기스): Traditional Korean fermentation uses sprouted barley malt enzymes (yeotgireum) to break down rice starches into sweet maltose. Because barley contains hordein (a gluten protein), barley malt triggers Celiac disease even when the FDA "Contains: Wheat" line does not mention barley!

Under US FDA labeling laws, manufacturers are required to declare Wheat in the bold "Contains" statement, but Barley and Rye are NOT required in the bold FDA allergen summary—meaning Celiac shoppers must always scan the full ingredient paragraph for barley malt, malt extract, or fermented grain.

3. How to Read Korean Ingredient Labels for Hidden Gluten (밀, 보리, 엿기름)

When shopping at H Mart, Lotte Plaza, Zion Market, or 99 Ranch, imported tubs sometimes place a US sticker over the Korean nutrition panel. If you look at the Korean allergen bar (알레르기 유발물질), memorize these four Korean words:

  • 밀 (Mil) = Wheat (Must be avoided by Celiac & wheat-allergy shoppers)
  • 보리 (Bori) / 대맥 (Daemaek) = Barley
  • 엿기름 (Yeotgireum) / 맥아 (Maega) = Barley Malt (Hidden gluten enzyme!)
  • 현미 (Hyeonmi) / 쌀 (Ssal) = Brown Rice / White Rice (100% Safe & Gluten-Free)

4. Gochujang vs. Gochugaru: The 5-Minute DIY Celiac-Safe Gochujang Recipe

Never confuse Gochujang (fermented chili paste) with Gochugaru (100% sun-dried Korean red chili flakes). Pure Gochugaru contains only one ingredient—dried red peppers—making it 100% naturally Gluten-Free, Vegan, Halal, and Top-9 Allergen Free!

If your local grocery store only stocks regular wheat-containing gochujang, you can whisk together a 5-minute Celiac-safe Gochujang substitute using pantry staples:

  • 3 tbsp 100% pure Korean Gochugaru chili flakes (shop Taekyung Gochugaru on Amazon)
  • 2 tbsp Certified Gluten-Free Miso or GF Doenjang (chickpea or rice miso works for soy-free!)
  • 2 tbsp Maple syrup, honey, or brown rice syrup
  • 1 tbsp Gluten-Free Tamari soy sauce + warm water to reach a glossy paste consistency

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Chung Jung One O'Food Gochujang really Celiac-safe?

Yes. Chung Jung One O'Food Gluten-Free Gochujang is specifically formulated for North American Celiac consumers using fermented brown rice instead of wheat and rice syrup instead of barley malt, testing below 20 ppm gluten.

Does Trader Joe's Gochujang contain gluten?

Yes, most Trader Joe's gochujang pastes and Korean-inspired sauces contain wheat flour or wheat-brewed soy sauce. Always check the bold 'Contains: Wheat' statement on Trader Joe's tubs and swap to O'Food or Sempio Gluten-Free Gochujang if you have Celiac disease.

Is Korean Gochugaru (red pepper flakes) gluten-free?

Yes! 100% pure Korean Gochugaru (고춧가루) consists solely of dried, coarse-ground red chili peppers with zero wheat or barley additives.
