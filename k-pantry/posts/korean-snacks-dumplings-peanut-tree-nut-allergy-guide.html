1. Top 5 Hidden Peanut & Tree-Nut Traps in Korean Food & Street Snacks

Compared to Southeast Asian cuisines, savory Korean soups and stews rarely use peanuts. However, Korean street snacks, cold noodles, and bakery mixes hide peanuts and tree nuts in unexpected places. Watch out for these 5 high-risk items:

  1. Boxed Korean Hotteok (Sweet Filled Pancake) Mixes (호떡믹스): Almost every boxed Hotteok mix sold at H Mart includes crushed roasted peanuts (땅콩분태) mixed directly into the brown sugar-cinnamon filling packet!
  2. Korean Fried Chicken (Yangnyeom Dakgangjeong / 양념치킨): Authentic sweet-and-spicy Korean glazed fried chicken is traditionally garnished with crushed peanuts or cooked in peanut-blended frying oil.
  3. Restaurant Ssamjang & Cold Soy Milk Noodles (Kongguksu / 콩국수): Many restaurants blend peanut butter, pine nuts (잣), or walnuts (호두) into house-made ssamjang dipping paste and chilled summer soy noodle broth for creaminess.
  4. Black Bean Noodle Oil Packets (Jajangmyeon / Chapagetti): Always check the finishing oil sachet in black bean noodles for peanut oil.
  5. Pepero, Hodugwaja & Gangjeong Snack Aisles: Even plain chocolate Pepero sticks are manufactured on shared lines with Almond and Peanut Pepero.

3. Sesame Oil vs. Perilla Oil (들기름): Navigating the FDA 9th Major Allergen

Since the US FASTER Act took effect, Sesame (참깨) must be declared on all US-distributed food labels. Because toasted sesame oil (chamgireum) is brushed onto almost all Korean kimbap, bibimbap, japchae, and bulgogi, sesame-allergic diners should stock Korean Roasted Perilla Seed Oil (들기름 / Deulgireum) at home. Perilla seeds come from an herb in the mint family (Perilla frutescens), offering a rich, toasted Korean aroma with zero sesame proteins!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do Korean dumplings (Mandu) contain peanuts or tree nuts?

Major US-sold Korean dumplings like Bibigo Steamed Dumplings and Beef Bulgogi Mandu do NOT contain peanuts or tree nuts in their recipe, though they do contain Wheat, Soy, and Sesame oil.

Does Lotte Milkis soda contain dairy?

Yes! Even though Milkis is a carbonated soft drink, it contains real nonfat dry milk (skim milk powder) and soybean polysaccharide, so it is not safe for dairy or soy allergies.

Why do Korean pancake mixes (Hotteok) have a peanut warning?

Traditional Korean sweet street pancakes (Hotteok) include finely chopped roasted peanuts inside the brown sugar-cinnamon filling packet for crunch. Always check Hotteok mixes for peanuts (땅콩).
