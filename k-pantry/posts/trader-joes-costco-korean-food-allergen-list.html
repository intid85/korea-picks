3. 4 Surprising Label Facts Every US Shopper Needs to Know

  1. Trader Joe's Kimbap Is Vegan (No Fish Cake!): Traditional Korean street kimbap uses ham, egg, and fish cake (eomuk). Trader Joe's uses braised fried tofu (yubu) and burdock root, making it 100% Vegan and Shellfish-Free!
  2. Trader Joe's Frozen Japchae Has Wheat: Even though sweet potato glass noodles are naturally gluten-free, TJ's seasons the frozen stir-fry with regular wheat-brewed soy sauce.
  3. Costco Jongga Kimchi vs. TJ's Kimchi: Costco's Jongga tub contains real shrimp and anchovy, whereas Trader Joe's refrigerated kimchi is 100% vegan and shellfish-free.
  4. Bibigo Dumplings Are Peanut & Tree-Nut Free: Great news for nut-allergy households—Bibigo's US dumpling facilities clearly declare Wheat, Soy, and Sesame, with no peanut ingredients in standard savory mandu.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Trader Joe's Kimbap have fish or shellfish?

No! Trader Joe's Frozen Kimbap is 100% vegan and contains zero fish cake or shellfish. The savory strip inside is braised tofu (yubu) seasoned with soy sauce (wheat, soy) and sesame oil.

Is Trader Joe's Japchae gluten-free?

No. Although the sweet potato glass noodles themselves are naturally gluten-free, the sauce used in Trader Joe's frozen Japchae contains regular wheat-brewed soy sauce.

Are Costco Bibigo dumplings nut-free?

Yes, Costco's Bibigo Steamed Dumplings and Beef Bulgogi Mandu are free of peanuts and tree nuts, though they do contain Wheat, Soy, and Sesame.
