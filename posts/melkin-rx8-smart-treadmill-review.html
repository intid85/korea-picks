Why Korean Apartments Need a Quiet Treadmill

Most Koreans live in apartments. Footsteps and motor noise travel easily to the neighbors below, and "floor noise" is a real social issue. That's why home treadmills sold in Korea compete on low noise and easy storage. The Melkin Smart Treadmill RX8 from Korean fitness brand Melkin Sports was built for this.
