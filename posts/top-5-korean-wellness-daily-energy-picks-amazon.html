In South Korea, rather than reaching for a third cup of sugary iced coffee at 3 PM, office workers and parents rely on slow-simmered traditional herbal extract pouches (Jin-aek) and 6-year Korean Red Ginseng sticks. What Goes Inside a Traditional Korean Herbal & Red Ginseng Extract

  • 6-Year Cultivated Root Standard: Korean ginseng reaches peak active ginsenoside (Rg1, Rb1, Rg3) density only in its sixth year of growth before being steamed and dried into Red Ginseng (Hongsam).
  • Complementary Botanical Roots: Traditional Korean tonic pouches often pair red ginseng with Angelica gigas (Danggui), Astragalus root (Hwanggi), Peony root, and sweet Korean jujube dates to balance the earthy bitterness with a smooth finish.
  • Zero Preservatives or Artificial Caffeine: High-temperature retort pouch sterilization locks in natural polyphenols without synthetic additives.
