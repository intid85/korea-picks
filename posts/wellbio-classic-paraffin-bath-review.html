How to Use a Paraffin Bath

  1. Melt the wax: Put the wax in the tub and switch it on. Follow the manual for melting time and temperature.
  2. Clean and dry your hands: Remove any rings first.
  3. Dip: Dip your hand in and lift it out so a thin, warm layer coats it. Repeat as the manual directs.
  4. Rest: Let the layer set for a few minutes. Many people wrap their hand to keep the warmth in.
  5. Peel: Peel the wax off. About 10 minutes makes a relaxing daily routine.
